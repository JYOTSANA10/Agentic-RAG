# Company IT Support Knowledge Base

## VPN Setup Guide
To connect to the company VPN from home:
1. Open Cisco AnyConnect Secure Mobility Client on your company laptop.
2. Enter the server address: pn.internal.company.com.
3. Click Connect and sign in with your corporate SSO credentials (email and password).
4. Approve the 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) push notification on your Duo Mobile app.
5. Once connected, the icon will turn green with a closed padlock.

## Password Reset Instructions
If you forgot your password or need a reset:
1. Visit https://auth.internal.company.com/self-service.
2. Click "Forgot My Password".
3. Enter your employee email address and confirm with your backup SMS or authenticator code.
4. Set a new password meeting the 14-character minimum requirement.
